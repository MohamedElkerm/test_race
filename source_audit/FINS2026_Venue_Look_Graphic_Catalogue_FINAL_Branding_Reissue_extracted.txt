Reissue checklist before final production
This is the operational bridge from concept visuals to approved event assets.
01
Confirm final public wording
Remove “postponed” from any graphic once official title is confirmed.
02
Lock logo files
Use original event/FISU assets only; no redraw or recreated gradient.
03
Approve sponsor matrix
Replace sponsor placeholders only after written approval.
04
Measure venue surfaces
Final dimensions for facade, towers, FoP boards and videoboard frame.
05
Test broadcast views
Check camera plan, glare, logo readability and clean field of play.
06
Export master assets
PPTX catalogue, print PDFs, PNG previews, editable production package.
Final deliverable: a venue-look-led graphic catalogue suitable for approval, design handoff and supplier briefing.
